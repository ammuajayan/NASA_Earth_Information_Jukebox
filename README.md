# 🌍 HEARTBEAT OF EARTH – The Earth Information Jukebox

### Turning Earth's scientific data into a musical experience.

---

## 🚀 About the Project

**What if we could listen to the changes happening on our planet?**

HEARTBEAT OF EARTH is an interactive web project that brings together Earth science, music, and storytelling.

We created this project to find a more interesting way for people to explore our planet and understand the environmental changes happening around us.

Through our website, users can explore Earth observations, discover important environmental events, learn about climate changes, and experience the connection between scientific information and music.

Our idea is simple: we don't just want people to see what is happening to Earth through numbers and graphs. We want them to connect with the story behind the data.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🌎 Interactive 3D Earth
- Explore our planet through an interactive 3D globe.
- Discover observation locations.
- Explore Earth-related information.

### 🎵 Earth Information Jukebox
- Explore music connected to important Earth events.
- Discover the stories behind scientific milestones.
- Experience environmental information through music.

### 📊 Climate Data Exploration
- Explore global temperature anomalies.
- Discover atmospheric CO₂ levels.
- Understand historical sea-ice changes.
- Explore environmental information through visualizations.

### 🛰️ NASA Mission and Event Exploration
- Discover NASA's contributions to Earth observation.
- Explore important scientific events and milestones.
- Learn about the role of satellite observations in understanding our planet.

### 🎧 Data-Inspired Soundscapes
- Connect scientific information with musical experiences.
- Explore creative ways of representing environmental changes through sound.

### 📱 Responsive Design
- Designed for desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.
- Simple and interactive user experience.

---

## 💡 Why We Built It

We often come across environmental information in the form of scientific reports, charts, and numbers. Although this information is important, it may not always be interesting or easy for everyone to understand.

We wanted to try something different.

By combining science with music, interactive visuals, and storytelling, we hope to make learning about Earth more engaging, especially for students and young people.

## 🎯 Our Goal and Intended Impact

Our goal is to make people more curious about our planet and more aware of the environmental changes taking place around us.

We hope to:
- Make Earth science easier and more interesting to explore.
- Encourage students to learn about climate change.
- Connect scientific information with creative experiences.
- Increase awareness of the importance of protecting our planet.

We believe that science can reach more people when it is presented in creative and accessible ways.
